# PowerBI-Sales-Performance-Dashboard
# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard

## Data Preparation
The data was reviewed and prepared before creating the dashboard to make sure the analysis and visuals were accurate.

# About the Project

I created this Power BI dashboard to explore sales data and get a better understanding of overall business performance.
The dashboard helps look at sales trends, products, regions, customer segments, and shipping performance in one place.

# What I Analyzed

* Total Sales and Profit
* Average Daily Sales
* Monthly Sales Trends
* Sales by Region
* Sales by Customer Segment
* Top Products and Sub-Categories
* Profit by Category
* Delivery Time by Ship Mode
* Profit and Discount relationship

# Business Impact

This dashboard can help a business:

* Identify strong and weak-performing regions
* Understand which products generate more sales
* Track changes in sales over time
* Compare different customer segments
* Review shipping performance and delivery times
* Understand how discounts are related to profit

# Tools Used

* Power BI
* DAX
* Data Visualization
